Privacy.

Updated 17 September 2026

This website

The project website has no analytics, cookies, advertising, account system, or third-party scripts. Its hosting provider may retain ordinary request logs under that provider's own policy.

The app and phone page

  • Movement and face detection happen on the Mac. There is no identity recognition or cloud vision.
  • While armed, a photo of anyone who looks at the Mac is shown on its lock screen and sent to your connected phone. That photo is deleted from the Mac's wallpaper folder when you disarm.
  • The microphone is never captured.
  • Alerts, photos, video clips, and commands travel only between your Mac and your iPhone over the local network, encrypted with the secret in the pairing QR code. No crookcooked server or third party is involved.
  • The pairing secret is stored on the Mac in a file only your user account can read, and in the browser storage of the phone that scanned the code. It travels in the link's # fragment, which browsers do not send over the network.
  • Evidence clips are saved on the Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/crookcooked/Evidence. Recent alerts are kept in memory until the app quits.

Your responsibility

You choose where the software runs and who can scan its code. Camera and recording laws vary; use evidence capture only where lawful and provide any notice your location requires.

Privacy.

Updated 16 September 2026

This website

The project website has no analytics, cookies, advertising, account system, or third-party scripts. Its hosting provider may retain ordinary request logs under that provider's own policy.

The apps

  • Camera scene and face-direction analysis happen on the Mac. There is no identity recognition or cloud vision.
  • The microphone is never captured.
  • Camera evidence is encrypted on the Mac before relay transport.
  • The pairing secret is stored in Apple Keychain on the Mac, and in the browser storage of the phone that scanned the code. It travels in the link's # fragment, which browsers do not send to the relay.

The self-hosted relay

The relay cannot decrypt camera evidence and does not receive the pairing secret. It does process an opaque room identifier, connection metadata, status, event type, and optional APNs device tokens. It also serves the phone web page, which loads no third-party code. Retained status and encrypted evidence are held in memory for up to one hour after both clients disconnect.

Your responsibility

You choose where the software and relay run. Camera and recording laws vary; use evidence capture only where lawful and provide any notice your location requires.

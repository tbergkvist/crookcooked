# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and mindful that security and privacy reports may involve stressful situations. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private data, and sustained disruptive behavior are not welcome.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security contact; do not expose personal information in a public issue. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and the safety of everyone involved.
